# Alpha Relay Operations

This document supplements the Alpha Relay Topology document. Gamma Endpoint is the final endpoint reached from Alpha Relay through Bridge Junction; it is not a direct Alpha Relay neighbor.

Gamma Endpoint retains delivery events for 14 days. Extending Gamma Endpoint retention requires Privacy Board approval. Approval from Platform North alone does not authorize a retention extension.

Platform North owns Bridge Junction and approves rollout of the Alpha Relay route. Before that rollout, Gamma Endpoint must have a passing restore drill completed within the preceding 48 hours. A passing drill older than 48 hours does not satisfy the rollout condition.

This corpus does not identify a named human approver for Gamma Endpoint retention. Team ownership must not be converted into an invented person's name.
